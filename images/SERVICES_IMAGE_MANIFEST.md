Image manifest for service photos

Instructions:
- Search the provided sites using the exact search queries listed below.
- Choose an image that fits the "treatment/process" style (illustration/3D where requested).
- Download the full-resolution image and save it under `public/images/services/` with the filename shown.
- Confirm license on each image page (Pexels/Unsplash/Pixabay are usually free for commercial use, but verify).

Recommended searches & filenames:

1. Root Canal Treatment
   - filename: root-canal.jpg
   - search on: Pexels -> "root canal 3d illustration" or Unsplash
   - prefer: 3D illustration / medical diagram (not patient photo)

2. Teeth Whitening
   - filename: teeth-whitening.jpg
   - search on: Pexels -> "teeth whitening treatment" or Unsplash
   - prefer: clinic treatment photo or patient with whitening shield

3. Braces & Aligners
   - filename: braces-aligners.jpg
   - search on: Pexels -> "dental braces close up" or "clear aligners smile"
   - prefer: close-up of braces or clear aligner fitting

4. Dental Implants
   - filename: dental-implants.jpg
   - search on: Unsplash -> "dental implants 3d illustration" or Pexels
   - prefer: 3D medical illustration of implant or clinical photo of implant prosthesis

5. Cosmetic Dentistry
   - filename: cosmetic-dentistry.jpg
   - search on: Pexels -> "cosmetic dentistry smile" or Unsplash
   - prefer: before/after style or procedure shot (non-identifiable patient if real photo)

6. Oral Surgery
   - filename: oral-surgery.jpg
   - search on: Pexels -> "oral surgery dental x ray" or Pixabay
   - prefer: surgical environment or dental x-ray/CBCT imagery

Helpful search pages:
- Pexels dental: https://www.pexels.com/search/dental/
- Pexels braces: https://www.pexels.com/search/dental%20braces/
- Pexels root canal: https://www.pexels.com/search/dental%20root%20canal%20treatment/
- Unsplash implants: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/dental-implants
- Unsplash orthodontics: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/orthodontics
- Unsplash whitening: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/teeth-whitening
- Pixabay dental: https://pixabay.com/photos/search/dental/

After placing the files, run `npm run build` to include them in the production build.
